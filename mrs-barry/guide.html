How to Use Your Trading Assistant

This page explains the difference between Pepperstone and MetaTrader 5, how the demo robot is intended to work, how capital and profit are tracked, and what must remain under your personal control.

← Back to Mrs. Barry Dashboard

1. Pepperstone and MT5 are not the same thing

Pepperstone is the broker relationship/account through which eligible markets, deposits, withdrawals and trading access are provided. MetaTrader 5 (MT5) is trading software/platform technology that can connect to a broker account.

Pepperstone — advantages

  • Your broker account, funding and withdrawals are managed through the broker.
  • Access to multiple CFD market categories can include forex, indices, commodities, shares, crypto CFDs and more, depending on account/region.
  • You can choose a supported platform such as MT5.

Pepperstone — watch out for

  • CFDs can involve leverage and losses can happen quickly.
  • Funding methods, currencies and instruments vary by region/account.
  • Always use the official broker site/app for deposits and withdrawals.

2. MetaTrader 5 — what it is

MT5 is a multi-asset trading platform. It can show charts, orders, positions, indicators and can run Expert Advisors (trading robots). Real trading through MT5 still requires a broker account.

MT5 — advantages

  • Strong charting and many timeframes.
  • Supports automated strategies / Expert Advisors.
  • Can be used on desktop, web and mobile depending on broker setup.

MT5 — watch out for

  • MT5 itself is not the company holding your trading funds.
  • A generic demo terminal is not the same as your Pepperstone live account.
  • Always confirm the broker/server/account before placing a real trade.

3. How Mrs. Barry should work when fully connected

1 · Monitor
The robot watches the markets you activate.
2 · Decide
It checks signal strength, spread, available capital and risk rules.
3 · Enter
If a valid setup exists, it can open a position in the allowed mode. If no setup is good enough, it waits.
4 · Protect
The robot manages Stop Loss, profit targets, trailing protection and opposite-signal exits.
5 · Record
Every simulated or real future action should be logged: what happened, why, and the result.
6 · Withdraw
The robot may notify you when a profit target is reached, but you personally perform the withdrawal in the broker portal.

4. Capital, profit and loss

Strategy Capital is the amount you permit the robot to use as its base. Cash Reserve stays intentionally unused. Profit Vault stores realised demo profit separately when automatic reinvestment is off.

Example: Strategy Capital = $100, Reserve = $25. The robot can only allocate from the remaining permitted amount, within the position limits you choose.

A profit target is not a guarantee. The robot must be allowed to say WAIT and invest nothing when conditions are not suitable.

5. When can the robot close a trade?

  • Risk exit: Stop Loss is reached.
  • Profit exit: Profit target is reached and the configured rule closes the trade.
  • Trailing protection: A profitable trade continues, but closes if price falls back too far from its best level.
  • Opposite signal: A strong opposite signal can close the existing position.
  • Emergency protection: Portfolio or daily loss rules can block or close trades.
  • Owner close: You may close a position manually.

SELL NOW can mean opening a short CFD trade. That is different from simply closing an existing BUY position.

6. Funding in Kenya / M-PESA

If M-PESA is available for your Pepperstone account/region, use the official Pepperstone funding area to choose it. Your dashboard can remember “M-PESA” as a preference, but it should never store your M-PESA PIN, bank credentials, card number, broker password or 2FA code.

Your broker account may still be denominated in a currency such as USD even when your personal display currency is KES. Currency conversion may therefore apply.

7. What this current demo already does

  • Shows more than seven example markets in a Market Library.
  • Lets you choose which markets the robot monitors.
  • Generates simulated BUY / WAIT / SELL decisions.
  • Can open and close simulated positions automatically.
  • Uses Stop Loss, profit targets, trailing protection and opposite signals.
  • Tracks Profit Vault, realised loss, open P&L and total result.
  • Creates a Robot History explaining entries, exits, profits and losses.

8. What is still required for real trading

The public HTML cannot safely place Pepperstone orders by itself. A real version needs a secure backend plus an MT5 Expert Advisor or another supported broker integration, account/server configuration, secure authentication, testing and explicit live activation.

Live trading must stay locked until the demo connection and safety rules have been tested thoroughly.
