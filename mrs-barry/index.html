Robot History

Short diary of what the robot did and why

Pepperstone vs MT5

Pepperstone is the broker relationship and account. MetaTrader 5 is a trading platform that can connect to a Pepperstone account. MT5 itself is not the broker holding the account.

Funding & Withdrawals

Funding methods depend on the Pepperstone account and region. For Kenya, M-PESA can be relevant. The robot should never store bank, card, M-PESA PIN or 2FA credentials.

How Profit Is Handled

In this demo, realised profit goes into the Profit Vault. The base strategy capital remains separate unless reinvestment is turned on. Real withdrawals stay manual in the broker portal.

When the Robot Buys

The robot compares active markets, signal strength, spread, available capital and risk limits. If no setup is strong enough, doing nothing is a valid decision.

When the Robot Sells / Closes

An existing position can close because of Stop Loss, profit target, trailing protection, a strong opposite signal, emergency risk protection, or an owner manual close.

What “SELL NOW” Means

For CFD trading, SELL can mean opening a short position. Closing an existing BUY position is different. Mrs. Barry shows these separately to avoid confusion.
